VigilPay

GenAI-powered scam prevention intelligence layer

This prototype demonstrates how VigilPay sits on top of an existing banking payment journey to detect scam risk, coach the customer, escalate high-risk cases and maintain a reconstructable audit trail.

Bank Transaction → Risk Assessment → RAG Scam Intelligence → Customer Coaching → Human Specialist → Audit → Pilot Validation
Prototype notice: The transaction, customer, scam intelligence and validation metrics used in this demonstration are synthetic. The mock LLM simulates the conversational GenAI component.
1 Customer Transfer & AI Coaching
Start with a synthetic S$25,000 investment transfer. VigilPay assesses the transaction, retrieves approved scam intelligence and asks the customer a short sequence of coaching questions.
