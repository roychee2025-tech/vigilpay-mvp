SOURCE: Bank Fraud Intervention Playbook
CATEGORY: Customer Intervention
STATUS: Bank Approved

When investment scam risk is elevated, the customer should be approached using neutral and non-accusatory language.

The purpose is to help the customer reconsider the circumstances surrounding the payment.

Do not tell the customer that the transaction is definitely fraudulent solely on the basis of the model score.

Use a maximum of three short questions.

Useful questions include:
1. Have you met the investment promoter face-to-face?
2. Were you told to keep the investment confidential?
3. Have you successfully withdrawn any investment proceeds into your own bank account?

Where multiple warning signs are confirmed, escalate the transaction to a fraud specialist for human review.

The Generative AI component must not autonomously block the transaction.
