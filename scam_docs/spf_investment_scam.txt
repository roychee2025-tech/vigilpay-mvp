SOURCE: SPF Investment Scam Advisory
CATEGORY: Investment Scam
STATUS: Approved knowledge-base record

Common warning signs include investment opportunities introduced through social media, messaging platforms or online-only relationships.

Victims may be promised unusually high or guaranteed returns.

Scammers may pressure victims to transfer money urgently.

Victims may be asked to make repeated transfers to new or unfamiliar accounts.

A significant warning sign is when the victim has not been able to withdraw investment proceeds successfully.

Recommended intervention:
Ask whether the customer has met the investment promoter in person.
Ask whether unusually high or guaranteed returns were promised.
Ask whether the customer has successfully withdrawn any investment proceeds.
