SOURCE: ScamShield Investment Scam Pattern
CATEGORY: Investment Scam
STATUS: Approved knowledge-base record

Investment scams may begin through online friendships, social-media messages or private chat groups.

The scammer may build trust before introducing an investment opportunity.

The customer may be shown apparent profits on a website or trading platform.

The displayed profits may not represent real investment returns.

Victims may be asked to make additional payments before withdrawals can be processed.

Recommended intervention:
Ask how the customer first came to know the person offering the investment.
Ask whether the relationship has only taken place online.
Ask whether investment profits have actually been transferred back into the customer's own bank account.
