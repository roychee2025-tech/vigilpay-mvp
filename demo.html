VigilPay

GenAI-Powered Scam Prevention Intelligence Layer

VigilPay demonstrates how conversational AI can complement a bank's existing fraud and payment controls by detecting elevated scam risk, retrieving approved scam intelligence, coaching the customer and escalating higher-risk cases to a human fraud specialist.

Bank Transaction → Risk Assessment → RAG Scam Intelligence → Customer Coaching → Human Specialist → Audit → Pilot Validation
Prototype notice: The transaction, customer, scam intelligence and validation metrics used in this demonstration are synthetic. The prototype uses local RAG retrieval and a mock LLM conversation to demonstrate the end-to-end architecture and control flow.

VigilPay Design Principles

Risk model: determines how concerned the system should be.

RAG: retrieves relevant approved scam intelligence.

Conversational AI: asks neutral questions to uncover information that may not be visible from the transaction alone.

Human specialist: retains authority for material payment decisions.

Audit: preserves the decision history for governance and investigation.
