Why Separate the Components?

Risk model: How concerned should we be?
RAG: What approved and current scam information is relevant?
LLM: What is the most useful neutral question to ask the customer?
Human specialist: What action should the bank take?
Audit layer: Can we reconstruct why each decision was made?

Key Design Principle

VigilPay does not give the LLM direct authority to approve or block a payment.

The AI supports detection, evidence retrieval and customer coaching. Material payment decisions remain subject to bank policy and human oversight.
